# Theories

### T1 — Death in the Tale of the Three Brothers is Dumbledore
- kind: reading-beyond-text
- confidence: plausible
- status: open
- source: fan-community
- earliest-reference: —
- claim-1: the eldest brother, who wants an unbeatable wand, stands for Voldemort
- claim-2: the middle brother, who wants the dead back, stands for Snape
- claim-3: the youngest brother, who hides under the Cloak and greets Death as an old friend, stands for Harry
- claim-4: Death, who greets the youngest brother at the end, stands for Dumbledore
- evidence: DH:21 (DH ch.21, the tale); DH:24 (DH ch.24, Voldemort takes the Elder Wand); DH:33 (DH ch.33, Snape's love for Lily); DH:34 (DH ch.34, Harry walks into the forest)
- falsifier: a canon statement that the tale's figures stand for no one, or for someone else

A widely shared reading whose origin has not been traced. Each brother's wish matches one
character's, and the youngest brother's calm end matches Harry's walk into the forest.

### T2 — Lily Potter is not dead, but held in stasis
- kind: reading-beyond-text
- confidence: speculative
- status: open
- source: example
- earliest-reference: —
- claim-1: Lily Potter did not die at Godric's Hollow; she is held in stasis at Potter Manor
- claim-2: the wand gives up James's echo before Lily's because only James died
- evidence: GoF:34 (GoF ch.34, first printings: the father's echo emerges first)
- falsifier: a canon account of Lily's death or burial

An illustrative theory, not a real one, written to show a theory resting on an erratum.
Its only evidence is the first printings' order of the echoes, which later printings
corrected.
