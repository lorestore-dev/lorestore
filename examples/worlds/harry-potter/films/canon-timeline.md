# The ruler: adaptation map

The films are dated on the books' ruler (§4.9). Each film adapts a range of book nodes. A
point such as `PS:6:2+00:41:10` means book scene `PS:6:2`, at that time in the film that
adapts it. The book path decides the order; the timecode only orders rows that share the
same path.
