# The ruler: inserted works

The animated series has episodes of its own, so it adds a work to the base's ruler
(§4.10). Its seasons are inserted after The Original Series' third season, placing it in
the last part of the five-year mission, before The Motion Picture. The points `TAS:…`
are valid only in a stack that includes this package.
