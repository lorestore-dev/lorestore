# Technology

### transporter — Transporter
- effect: converts a person or object to energy and rematerializes it elsewhere
- practice: routine crew transport; malfunctions are rare but dramatic, e.g. an ion storm sends a landing party to a parallel universe (TOS "Mirror, Mirror")
- definition: partial
- cost: ship's power
- limit: cannot beam through raised deflector shields; limited range
- as-of: TOS
- source: canon
- citation: TOS, recurring

### warp-drive — Warp drive
- effect: faster-than-light travel
- practice: speed is given as a warp factor; pushing beyond rated speed strains the engines
- definition: partial
- cost: antimatter fuel and dilithium crystals
- limit: rated maximum warp factor of the ship
- as-of: TOS
- source: canon
- citation: TOS, recurring
