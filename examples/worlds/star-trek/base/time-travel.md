# Time travel

Abridged: one mechanism. Star Trek has many, and they do not all follow the same model.

### temporal-rift — Temporal rift near Narendra III
- model: mutable
- direction: both
- reach: links two fixed moments, 2344 and 2366
- carries: body; memory; the ship and everything aboard
- free-will: everyone
- immune: —
- effect: a ship that passes through arrives at the rift's other end, and its absence from its own time changes the history that follows
- practice: the Enterprise-C's disappearance from 2344 replaces the familiar 2366 with one at war with the Klingons; its return through the rift restores the original history
- cost: —
- limit: a ship going back through the rift arrives at the moment it left
- as-of: TNG:3:15
- source: canon
- citation: TNG "Yesterday's Enterprise"
