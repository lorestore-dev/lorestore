## Divergence conflicts

### C1 — harry-potter.glasses (D1: Harry is blind)
- canon: PS:2 "round"
- option keep: he wears glasses anyway (for appearance, or the Dursleys insist). Fallout: needs explaining.
- option adapt: dark glasses. Fallout: a visual signature others react to.
- option remove: no glasses. Fallout: Dudley's mockery targets something else.
- option substitute: a cane or a guide instead. Fallout: changes how he moves through every scene.

### C2 — harry-potter.role (D1: Harry is blind)
- canon: PS:9 "Gryffindor Seeker" (McGonagall sees him fly and recruits him)
- option keep: he is made Seeker anyway. Fallout: the story must make a blind Seeker credible.
- option adapt: McGonagall sees him fly by sound and feel. Fallout: his flying becomes a defining skill.
- option remove: he is never on the team. Fallout: no Nimbus Two Thousand (PS:10); Quidditch leaves his story.
- option substitute: someone else is made Seeker. Fallout: a new rivalry or friendship.

### C3 — harry-potter.broom (D1: Harry is blind)
- canon: PS:10 "Nimbus Two Thousand"
- depends on: C2. Under keep or adapt it can stand; under remove or substitute it goes.
- option keep: the broom arrives as in canon.
- option remove: no broom.
