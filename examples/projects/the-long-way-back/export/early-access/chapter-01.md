# Narendra

The bridge shook again, and Mira held on to the rail. Captain Garrett called for
everything the shields had left, though there was nothing left to give them.

Ahead of them, the stars tore open.

* * *

She woke under dim red light, to a voice she did not know asking for her rank and
ship. The ship around her was an Enterprise, but it had been built for a war.
