# Premise

**Logline.** When the Enterprise-C goes back through the rift, Lieutenant Mira Oduya
stays behind: a woman from 2344 in the restored timeline of 2366, where everyone she
knew is twenty-two years older or dead.

**Central dramatic question.** Can she build a life in a future she was never meant to
see, without telling anyone how the war that never happened was stopped?
