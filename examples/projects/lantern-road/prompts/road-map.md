## Prompt
A traveller's map of a single road running north to south. At the top, Highcairn, a
hill town; at the bottom, Lowmere, a town in the marshes. Halfway between them, one
inn standing alone beside the road. The southern half of the road crosses the marsh
on a long causeway. The two halves
of the road are drawn about the same length. Muted inks on a pale paper, a compass rose,
hand-lettered names.

## Gaps
- The inn's look is not given: it is drawn as a small, plain building.
- The landscape around the road, beyond the marsh, is not given, and is left plain.
