# Adherence contract

## Canon in scope
*Goblet of Fire* in full (cutoff `GoF:37`), and the cast and institutions it
establishes. See `characters/harry-potter.md` and `characters/cedric-diggory.md` in the
world layer.

## Point of divergence
`GoF:32`, the graveyard. In canon Cedric is killed; here he survives (D1).

## Original additions
Mara Quill, a Ravenclaw in Cedric's year (`characters/mara-quill.md`).

## Fidelity
`flexible`. Minor forks are acceptable, but the rules of magic and the Ministry's
politics are load-bearing and must hold.
