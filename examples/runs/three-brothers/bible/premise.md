# Premise

## Logline

At King's Cross, in the white place between living and dying, a boy who chose to walk
to his death is greeted by an old friend, and the story he heard as a child turns out
to have been about the two of them all along.

## Central dramatic question

Will Harry, offered the rest that the tale's third brother took, choose to go back?

## Promised experience

The quiet of a last page. A reader who knows the tale of the three brothers reads the
King's Cross conversation again and hears it differently: Dumbledore speaks as the tale
casts Death, greeting the one brother who did not try to cheat him, as an equal. It is
told in the hush of a fable, close on Harry, with canon's events unchanged and only
their framing new. The feeling it leaves is recognition, then grief, then the choice to
go on.
